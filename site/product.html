Product

Search and verify European company data.

Company Data API connects your product or workflow to official company registries in Norway and the United Kingdom — with more countries on the way. One API, one auth header, predictable JSON responses.

See it live Check coverage
Official registry sources

Data comes from Brønnøysundregistrene in Norway and Companies House in the UK — not scraped, not aggregated from unknown sources.

Built for integration

REST API with consistent field naming, standard HTTP status codes, and response shapes that don't change without notice.

Use cases

Who uses it and why

KYC and compliance

Verify company registration numbers, legal names, and status before onboarding a business customer or partner.

CRM enrichment

Automatically fill in company details — address, industry, registration number — from a company name search.

B2B lead data

Look up companies by name to get verified registry information for sales and marketing workflows.

Capabilities

What the API gives you today

A narrow but reliable set of endpoints — useful for real integrations, not just demos.

Company search

Search by name or registration number. Filter by country. Get structured results with registration number, name, status, and address.

Country catalogue

Query which countries are active and what source status they carry. Use it to drive country pickers in your UI.

Registration number lookup

Resolve a registration number to a verified company record — useful for validation and enrichment flows.

Predictable contract

Consistent field names, standard error codes (401, 400, 404), and versioned routes under /v1.

Norway coverage

Live data from Brønnøysundregistrene. Company name, org number, address, industry code, and activity.

UK coverage

Live data from Companies House. Company name, registration number, status, address, and incorporation date.
