Platform shape

The public front door stays public

What belongs here

Product explanation, docs entry, coverage, indicative pricing, and a safe browser demo that helps technical buyers evaluate the API.

What does not

API key management, billing operations, ingestion runs, internal health control, or customer settings.

Why that matters

Visitors understand the offer faster when the public site is not also pretending to be the customer dashboard and operator console.
