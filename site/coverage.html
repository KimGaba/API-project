Coverage

Live now. More countries coming.

We start with Norway and the United Kingdom — two markets with strong open data access and reliable official registries. Denmark and Finland follow next.

Norway

Data from Brønnøysundregistrene. Covers company name, org number, address, industry code, and activity status.

NO · Live Brønnøysundregistrene
United Kingdom

Data from Companies House. Covers company name, registration number, status, address, and incorporation date.

GB · Live Companies House
Denmark

Next on the roadmap. Will source from CVR — the Danish Central Business Register.

DK · Planned
Finland

Planned for a later phase. Will source from the Finnish Business Information System (YTJ).

FI · Planned

Official sources only

Every country uses data from its official government registry — not aggregated or scraped from third parties.

Clear live vs. planned

No roadmap theatre. Countries are either live and queryable today, or explicitly marked as planned.

Consistent fields

Regardless of country, the same core fields — name, registration number, status, address — are present in the response.
